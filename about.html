About Col Strategy

Welcome to Col Strategy

A boutique consultancy crafting go-to-market and growth strategy for healthcare, health-tech, and other businesses innovating to make an impact on society.

Our blend of industry expertise, data-driven insight, and agile methodology equips clients to move toward their ambitions with speed and adaptability — the same practice formerly known as Agile Growth Strategies, now under a new name and mark.

Founder & Principal

Ryan

Col Strategy is built on Ryan's proven track record driving innovation and change across technology and healthcare — product, sales, operations, and engineering experience with industry leaders including Kaiser Permanente, Hearst Health, Amgen, and Intel, plus decades mentoring and partnering with startups and fellow consultants.

His cross-sector background spans healthcare, technology, informatics, public health, tribal health, rural health, data modernization, and health IT — with particular focus on underserved niches where good strategy is hardest to find and matters most.
